Privacy Policy

Benny's Great Escape · Last updated 2 November 2026

This app does not collect any data about you. There is no account to create, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app has no network connection of its own.

What is stored on your device

Four settings are saved locally so the game remembers them between sessions: your best score, whether music is on, and whether you've already seen the jump and slide hints. These never leave your device, are not readable by anyone else, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you choose to use the leaderboard, your score is submitted to Apple's Game Center. Apple keeps the scores and owns the player identities — this app never sees your Apple Account, your name, or anyone else's. That exchange is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, not this one. Game Center is optional; the game is fully playable without it.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Since nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub Issues.
