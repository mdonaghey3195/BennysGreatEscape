How to play

Swipe up to jump over logs, stumps and bushes. Swipe down to slide under benches. The run speeds up the longer you last, and the ground opens into gaps you'll need to clear.

Leaderboard

Scores are kept in Apple's Game Center. If the leaderboard doesn't appear, check that you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app, under your Apple Account.

Sound and music

Both can be turned off from the Settings button on the title screen.

Get in touch

Found a bug, or have an idea? Report it on GitHub Issues. You'll need a free GitHub account to post, and you can follow the issue there to see when it's fixed.
